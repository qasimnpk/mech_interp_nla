The text-side result supports greater sensitivity to the tested paraphrases than factual corruptions. The activation-side result does not establish the same ordering. The distinction is where you make the edit: text side — the explanation given to AR changes, the activation is fixed, question: does AR penalize an incorrect description more than a paraphrase; activation side — the source passage changes, producing a new activation and AV explanation, question: can AR match descriptions to the corresponding activations after different source edits. "Fact change" and "wording change" are not independent categories: a factual edit necessarily changes the text; the comparison asks whether changing meaning in addition to wording produces a distinguishable effect relative to a meaning-preserving edit; tokenization, frequency, associations and activation distance can differ. The activation-side point estimates favor stronger discrimination for the entity edit, but the paired difference is inconclusive; that neither reverses nor confirms the text-side result.
