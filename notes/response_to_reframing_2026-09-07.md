## 2. Consequences for the write-up (do these)

- **Finding 2 is a replication.** Dingeto already showed on this exact checkpoint that flipping a specific claim rarely moves the score (≈2% grounded) and that paraphrase keeps most of it. Cite it in the first paragraph of the finding and present ours as a confirmation with a different instrument: a *paired* same-claim comparison (corruption vs paraphrase of the same claim, cluster-bootstrap CIs) rather than a per-claim threshold against an empirical null; plus the every-claim version (R2) and the deterministic swaps. Also cite their Table 7 next to our S3/C1 numbers (their paraphrase 0.89 retained; our one-claim paraphrase costs 0.005 of 0.88; our reorder −0.026). Per Neel's FAQ a replication is acceptable when it is labelled and the novelty is elsewhere.
- **Finding 5 gains a convergent citation and a required control.** Their ridge ceiling (specifics ~6%, gist 18–50%) is the probe-side twin of our topic-strong / entity-weak readout. What is not in either paper: the released AV's own prefilled likelihood as the readout (their text has no prefill or likelihood readout; they use probes and logit lens), with donor swaps and a held-out prefix. The Solshine artifact makes a **base-model-with-injection control mandatory** before the 0.94 can be called learned activation reading: inject the same vector at the marker in Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct itself, same prefill, same candidates (T2a items and T2c pairs). Target-only, ≈1,500 forwards, ≈10 min.
- **Finding 3 is our clearest unclaimed contribution**, restated: the final-token feature is *designed in* (warm-start prompt), so its existence is unsurprising; what we add is that it carries 88% of the reconstruction lift, keeps 79% of its cost when moved to the front, and is the verifiably-about-the-model part of the explanation (quoted token right in 82%, predicted continuation hits the target's top-1 in 44%, vs 1.8% verbatim quotes elsewhere). Dingeto's decomposition is per claim by threshold; ours is per position with a reorder control. Not in either paper as far as the texts show.
- **Not in either paper, keep:** source-edit matched pairs on the released NLA (C2/C3); AV promptability and residual steering (S5/T3); injected-direction sensitivity (T4). Drop from the story: "syntax over semantics", any training-incentive claim, the round-4 "D-score" pooling across positions (their recurrence result already covers pooling).
- **Lead question (agree with the advisor):** what task-relevant information survives a released NLA's verbalization–reconstruction pipeline, and can targeted access recover what its standard interface misses?

## 3. Extension choice — recommendation

Both advisors agree on one bounded extension at most, no dependency for the submission. Ranked by certainty of a usable result per human hour:

1. **Base-model-with-injection control (mandatory, ≈10 min compute, 15 min human).** Without it the readout result is exposed to a known artifact. Kill: if base-model accuracy/AUROC ≥ AV on the same items → the readout is not learned; report either way.
2. **Conditional-snippet experiment (advisor's second choice; AR-only, ≈20 min compute, 30 min human).** Backgrounds: full explanation; snippet removed; a matched amount of other text removed (the first claim, or a random span of equal word count not overlapping the tested claim). For each accepted non-snippet triple score original, corrupted and paraphrased claim in each background (≈330 rows × 3 backgrounds × 3 texts ≈ 3,000 AR forwards). Primary: change in factual discrimination across backgrounds, as A − P and as AUROC(Δ corrupt vs Δ paraphrase), not the lower absolute score. Kill: CI of [(A − P)_no-snippet − (A − P)_full] ≤ 0 → MET (removing the snippet does not unmask factual sensitivity). Interpretation grid as the advisor wrote. Builds on our own 88% result; not found in either paper.
3. **Round-trip relational task (advisor's first choice = our round-4 proposal).** Highest ceiling: Dingeto's own limitation says patching the tap barely moves behavior, so "does the reconstructed state preserve what the model uses" is open. Cost: new AR→target injection with norm restoration, self-patch check, donor patches, matched-error perturbation, a competence gate; half a day elapsed if `src/patching.py` and `nla_lib` transfer, ≈2 h human. Risk: may not validate in time. Run only as a gated pilot (8 pairs) in parallel, promoted to evaluation only if the gates pass and the human approves raw examples; otherwise it stays the named future work.
4. Generalize-vs-delete (advisor's third; our N3): modest; only if 1–2 finish early.
